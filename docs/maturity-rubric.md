# Maturity rubric

What each control and evidence maturity level (1 to 5) actually means. Referenced by the maturity scores shown on the [control library](controls.md) and [risk register](risks.md). Generated from data/maturity-rubric.yaml.

*10 rows. Source: `exports/maturity-rubric.csv`.*

| Dimension | Level | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Control Maturity | 1 | Initial | Controls informal or inconsistently applied; no documented procedures; reactive; unclear ownership; no metrics. |
| Control Maturity | 2 | Developing | Controls partially documented but inconsistently followed; limited standardization; tooling exists but not fully configured; coverage gaps. |
| Control Maturity | 3 | Defined | Controls fully documented with clear owners; standardized and repeatable; tooling implemented but needs tuning; metrics defined but not consistently reviewed. |
| Control Maturity | 4 | Managed | Controls consistently implemented and monitored; evidence produced on a defined cadence; tooling integrated with alerting; KPIs/KRIs tracked; exceptions formally logged. |
| Control Maturity | 5 | Optimized | Controls automated where possible; continuous improvement cycle; predictive analytics; benchmarking; governance-driven enhancements. |
| Evidence Maturity | 1 | Initial | Evidence missing or incomplete; ad hoc collection; no versioning/timestamps; limited traceability. |
| Evidence Maturity | 2 | Developing | Evidence exists but inconsistent or outdated; manually collected; limited traceability. |
| Evidence Maturity | 3 | Defined | Evidence collected on a predictable schedule; standardized formats; versioning and timestamps present; lacks trend analysis. |
| Evidence Maturity | 4 | Managed | Evidence complete, accurate, and consistently maintained; automated or semi-automated collection; clear lineage; shows trend data. |
| Evidence Maturity | 5 | Optimized | Evidence fully automated and audit-ready; real-time dashboards; anomaly detection; strong governance linkage. |
