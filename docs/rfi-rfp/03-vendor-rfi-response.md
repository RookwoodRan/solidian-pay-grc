## 1. Vendor overview

Epictetus Assurance, Ltd. is an accredited management-systems certification body founded in 2016, with practice areas spanning ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 42001, and SOC 2 examination services for service organizations (SOC 2 attestation is performed by our affiliated CPA firm; readiness advisory is delivered through a firewalled advisory affiliate — see Section 7). We operate across North America and the UK with a team of 40 certified auditors and technical reviewers.

## 2. Relevant experience

Epictetus Assurance has completed 11 ISO/IEC 42001 certification engagements since the standard's 2023 publication, including three in payments/fintech, two in insurance, and one in digital lending. All engagement leads hold ISO/IEC 42001 and ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor credentials; our AI risk practice includes reviewers with applied ML backgrounds, and our SOC 2 practice has examined service organizations across all five Trust Services Criteria categories, including Processing Integrity engagements for payment processors.

## 3. Approach and methodology

Our model is **assurance-led, not implementation-led**: we review, challenge, and validate the AI management system Solidian Pay builds, rather than authoring it ourselves. This preserves our ability to serve as certification body for the same scope. Typical sequencing: independent gap pre-assessment → design/documentation review checkpoints → risk-and-control evidence sampling → readiness review → Stage 1 → corrective-action verification → Stage 2 → certification decision.

## 4. Fintech and AI-specific capabilities

Direct experience reviewing AI inventories covering fraud scoring, underwriting/credit decisioning, KYC document automation, and LLM-based customer support tooling — including third-party foundation-model oversight, human-oversight model validation, and transparency/model-card sufficiency review.

## 5. Leveraging an existing SOC 2 control environment

Where a client already maintains a mapped SOC 2 control set, we scope the AIMS engagement to avoid duplicating assurance already obtained. In Solidian Pay's case we would expect material reliance on existing Common Criteria controls (CC1–CC9) for the governance, access, change-management and vendor-oversight requirements of ISO/IEC 42001, allowing the engagement to concentrate on genuinely AI-specific clauses — AI inventory and risk classification, impact assessment, human oversight, data governance for AI, and AI-incident handling.

## 7. Certification support boundary

Epictetus Assurance maintains strict separation between any pre-certification advisory work (delivered only through a legally separate, firewalled affiliate with no shared engagement staff) and certification-decision functions. We will not draft Solidian Pay's policies, approve its risk treatments, or operate its controls.

## 8. Artefacts and accelerators

Reusable evidence trackers, annotated document-review checklists, interview guides, and a readiness scoring rubric — provided as accelerators for Solidian Pay's own team to use, not as authored management-system content.
