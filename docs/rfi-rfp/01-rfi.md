## 1. Procurement overview

Solidian Pay is a fictional growth-stage fintech company providing digital payments, merchant risk tools, embedded finance services, and AI-enabled operations tooling in North America and the United Kingdom. The company intends to mature its AI governance program and pursue certification to ISO/IEC 42001:2023.

This RFI gathers information about vendor capabilities, delivery models, relevant fintech experience, certification-support boundaries, indicative timelines, and pricing approaches. Responses inform a shortlist and a subsequent Request for Proposal (RFP). This RFI is not a commitment to award work, reimburse proposal costs, or enter into any agreement.

## 2. Company profile and operating context

| Topic | Details |
|---|---|
| Business Profile | B2B and B2B2C fintech platform supporting card and ACH payments, merchant onboarding, fraud controls, underwriting decision support, and regulated customer operations |
| Employees | Approximately 900 employees across engineering, data science, risk, compliance, operations, and customer support |
| Geography | Primary operations in the United States and Canada, with service delivery and support functions in the United Kingdom |
| Technology environment | Cloud-native stack combining internally developed ML services, third-party foundation-model integrations, vendor platforms, and conventional software systems |

## 3. Objectives of the RFI

- Identify firms with demonstrable experience implementing or supporting ISO/IEC 42001 programs, especially in regulated or high-trust industries.
- Understand available delivery models: gap assessment, control design, documentation support, implementation coaching, internal audit, and external certification readiness.
- Assess whether vendors can work effectively with a mixed environment of internally built and third-party AI-enabled solutions.
- Clarify how vendors manage independence constraints when they also hold relationships with certification bodies or affiliated audit entities.
- Estimate realistic timelines, team profiles, and pricing structures for an end-to-end engagement.

## 6. Preliminary assumptions for planning

- Solidian Pay expects the selected partner to work collaboratively with legal, risk, privacy, security, engineering, data science, product, compliance, and internal audit stakeholders.
- The company prefers an implementation approach that leverages existing governance structures rather than a standalone process stack.
- The engagement may require mapping AI governance activities to adjacent frameworks already familiar to the company — security controls, model-risk concepts, privacy requirements, and regulatory obligations.
- Some AI use cases are high-sensitivity: they may influence fraud decisions, onboarding outcomes, customer interactions, or regulatory operations.

## 9. Important conditions

- This RFI is exploratory and informal. Issuance does not obligate Solidian Pay to issue an RFP, select any respondent, or award any contract.
- Respondents must not state or imply that certification can be guaranteed. Certification, if pursued, must be awarded independently by a qualified certification body following its own audit procedures.
- Any respondent with certification-body affiliations, reseller arrangements, or other relationships that could create independence conflicts should disclose them clearly.
- Solidian Pay reserves the right to modify the procurement schedule, request follow-up information, or reject any or all responses.
