## 1. Purpose and intent

Following market discovery through an RFI, Solidian Pay intends to select a primary professional-services partner to design, implement, operationalize, and demonstrate conformance of an AI management system aligned to ISO/IEC 42001:2023.

The selected proposer should act as a practical implementation partner: assessing current state, closing gaps, building fit-for-purpose management-system documentation and evidence, enabling process owners, conducting internal audit support, and helping the company prepare for Stage 1 and Stage 2 certification audits.

The selected proposer will not be the entity that awards certification unless it is an accredited certification body and all independence requirements are demonstrably preserved. Proposers must explain how they will avoid conflicts of interest.

## 2. Company background and AI profile

| Topic | Details |
|---|---|
| Business model | Digital payments, fraud controls, embedded finance, and decision-support tools for merchants, platforms, and regulated operational teams |
| AI deployment profile | Predictive models, rules-plus-ML services, vendor AI capabilities, large-language-model integrations, internal copilots |
| Risk context | Several AI-enabled processes operate in customer-facing or regulated contexts and require robust governance, traceability, and oversight |

## 3. Scope of work

| Phase | Required activities |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 — Mobilization and current-state assessment | Confirm project governance, identify stakeholders, finalize AIMS scope, document review and interviews, assess current capability against ISO/IEC 42001, deliver prioritized gap assessment |
| Phase 2 — AIMS design and documentation | Develop/refine policy, scope statement, objectives, governance model, documented procedures, records framework, roles and responsibilities, management-review inputs, competence/awareness materials, document-control ownership |
| Phase 3 — Risk and control implementation | Establish AI inventory and classification approach; support AI risk assessment, AI system impact assessment and treatment planning; define lifecycle, monitoring, incident, transparency, third-party oversight, data, and human-oversight controls; align evidence expectations |
| Phase 4 — Enablement and operationalization | Train control owners, run working sessions, embed management reviews and reporting, define metrics/KPIs, support pilot operation |
| Phase 5 — Internal audit and corrective action support | Plan and execute internal audit / audit-ready package, document findings, support root-cause analysis, corrective actions, closure evidence |
| Phase 6 — Certification readiness and external audit support | Prepare for Stage 1/Stage 2 activities, coordinate evidence requests, facilitate mock interviews, support remediation, assist during certification-body interactions |

## 4. Specific deliverables

| Deliverable | Minimum content | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Project charter and implementation plan | Detailed timeline, governance, RAID log, dependencies, stakeholder map | Within 2 weeks of kickoff |
| ISO/IEC 42001 gap assessment | Current-state assessment, requirement-by-requirement observations, maturity view, remediation roadmap | End of Phase 1 |
| AIMS document set | Policy, scope, roles, risk methodology, procedures, records list, training materials, management-review inputs | Phases 2–3 |
| AI inventory and applicability framework | Inventory method, categorization model, evidence approach, oversight expectations for internal and third-party AI | Phase 3 |
| AI system impact assessment framework | Assessment methodology and template, trigger criteria, documentation and retention expectations | Phase 3 |
| Control implementation workpapers | Requirement interpretation, owner assignments, planned evidence, residual gaps | Phases 3–4 |
| KPI and reporting pack | Operational metrics, dashboard concepts, review cadence materials | Phase 4 |
| Internal audit package | Audit plan, test work, findings log, management response support | Phase 5 |
| Certification readiness package | Stage 1/2 readiness checklist, evidence index, interview prep materials, audit support playbook | Phase 6 |

## 5. Proposal requirements

- Describe your proposed approach, methods, tools, assumptions, and required client inputs for each phase.
- Provide a proposed project plan with estimated duration, milestones, and critical dependencies.
- Identify the engagement team, roles, credentials, and level of effort by phase — clearly distinguishing implementation resources from any internal audit / independent review resources.
- Explain your experience with AI governance in fintech, banking, payments, insurance, or adjacent regulated sectors.
- Provide at least three comparable references and summarize outcomes achieved.
- State all assumptions, exclusions, and optional items; list any third-party software or licenses required.
- Provide a pricing schedule with fixed-fee, milestone-based, or not-to-exceed structures; rate cards may back up a required project-commercial view.
- Disclose any affiliations with certification bodies and describe how independence and impartiality will be maintained.

## 9. Terms, assumptions and conditions

- Solidian Pay may reject any proposal, request clarifications, negotiate with one or more proposers, or cancel the procurement at any time.
- Proposal preparation costs are the sole responsibility of the proposer.
- Any award is subject to contract negotiation, vendor onboarding, legal review, security review, and approval of a final statement of work.
- Proposers must preserve the confidentiality of non-public information shared during the procurement.
- No proposer may claim to guarantee certification; certification decisions remain the responsibility of the selected certification body.
- Solidian Pay prefers reusable deliverables, knowledge transfer, and an operating model sustainable internally after project completion.
