## 2. Proposal summary

Epictetus Assurance proposes an assurance-led path that gives Solidian Pay a credible, efficient route to ISO/IEC 42001 certification while preserving the impartiality required of a certification body. Our value is not drafting policies on Solidian Pay's behalf; it is showing Solidian Pay exactly what an auditor and certification decision panel will require, early enough to avoid rework, delays, and avoidable nonconformities.

We propose a six-phase program aligned to the RFP structure, executed as independent review checkpoints, evidence validation, readiness assessments, and certification activities. This model is especially strong for fintech environments because it concentrates effort on scope clarity, AI inventory accuracy, supplier oversight, model-risk evidence, management-review inputs, and sampled operating evidence across product, security, privacy, compliance, and operations teams. Where the RFP requests implementation support, our proposal responds with review-and-challenge services rather than authorship of the management system, which keeps Solidian Pay eligible for independent certification by Epictetus Assurance for the same scope.

## 8. Assumptions, exclusions, and dependencies

This proposal assumes Solidian Pay retains responsibility for management-system design, policy authorship, control implementation, internal audit execution, and management decisions. Epictetus Assurance will review, challenge, and verify readiness, but will not act in a consulting capacity on the same AIMS it may certify.

Commercial assumptions: one primary legal entity, English-language records, up to 25 material AI use cases, remote documentation review by default, and timely access to process owners and evidence owners. Material scope expansion, additional locations, or major remediation work after Stage 1 may require repricing.

Certification remains contingent on objective evidence, closure of nonconformities, and an independent certification decision. No wording in this proposal should be read as a guarantee of certification.

## 10. Independence, conflicts, and commercial exceptions

Epictetus Assurance is proposing as the certification body and will maintain strict independence boundaries throughout this engagement. We will not draft Solidian Pay's policies, decide or approve risk treatment on Solidian Pay's behalf, operate controls, or serve as internal auditor of record for the same scoped management system.

Independence is preserved through client-acceptance conflict screening, separation of commercial, audit, technical review, and certification-decision functions, and documented quality controls over reviewer assignment and escalation. We are not aware of any existing conflicts that would prevent us from taking on the Solidian Pay scope.

**Commercial exception:** where the RFP uses implementation language, our proposal substitutes independent review-and-readiness services. We believe this is the strongest and most defensible model if Solidian Pay wants the same provider to remain eligible as its certification body.
